# Competition packaging checklist

Theme: **Assistive Technology & Accessibility**. Intended event: Hack for Social Cause under VBYLD 2027. Confirm current requirements directly in the portal.

- [ ] Verify actual deadline and team eligibility.
- [ ] Replace provisional state/campus and team information with true details.
- [ ] Review source and run the prototype from a clean checkout.
- [ ] Publish complete source with README, architecture, synthetic fixtures, tests and license.
- [ ] Use the official presentation template; prepare 6–7 slides, PPT/PDF no larger than 10 MB.
- [ ] Clearly distinguish working prototype, simulated actors/data, planned features and measured results.
- [ ] Include actual AI assistance and a defensible estimate of its extent.
- [ ] If included, video: 3–5 minutes, MP4/MOV, 720p or above, at most 80 MB.
- [ ] Optional design documents at most 10 MB.
- [ ] Prepare signed declaration and required self-attested student-ID attachments privately; one PDF at most 5 MB per supplied form.
- [ ] Check title ≤100 characters and alignment/problem/solution each ≤500 after final edits.
- [ ] Verify repository is publicly accessible and final upload confirmation is saved.

Do not commit completed declarations, student/government IDs or signatures. Gitignore exclusions are only a precaution, not proof that a file is safe to publish. The declaration PDF's age-reference date and public website differ; resolve relevant eligibility ambiguity with the organizer. Saving a draft is not proof of a final submission.
