# Four-minute demo outline

1. **0:00–0:30:** Team and problem, then the hook line: "No actor can close their own leg of the relay, and evidence must be re-passed before it goes stale." Identify all data as fictional.
2. **0:30–1:00:** Drag the **preview-time slider** live — watch a currently-clear segment flip from "Current observation" to "Needs a fresh check" in front of the judges. This is the core idea made visible, not just claimed.
3. **1:00–1:45:** Compare three journeys: barrier, stale observation and current observations. Explain no safety guarantee.
4. **1:45–2:30:** Staff claims the library ramp repair. Status remains pending. The same actor cannot recheck — stage the "gotcha": try rechecking as staff, show it's rejected.
5. **2:30–3:15:** Switch to Independent reviewer. Fail the recheck once (ramp reopens blocked), then pass it (ramp clears). The unobserved library desk still prevents a complete current journey.
6. **3:15–3:40:** Click "Read status aloud" — live speech-synthesis readout of the journey status, a real accessibility-tech moment, not a slide claim.
7. **3:40–4:00:** Explain local-only persistence, simulated identity, established alternatives, AI assistance and the proposed beneficiary/staff validation.

Do not invent user counts, prevented incidents, partnerships or audited compliance. Show real test output and describe what it does and does not verify.
